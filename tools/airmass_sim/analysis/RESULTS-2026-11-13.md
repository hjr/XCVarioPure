# AirMass at 10 Hz instead of 20 Hz, 2026-11-13

The device steps AirMass every 100 ms, with its sensors, instead of every 50 ms. The browser replay follows: it inserts
prediction ticks every 100 ms instead of 50 ms, and only where a frame is missing (more than 150 ms apart), so a 10 Hz
log replays one cycle per frame like the device. No estimator setting changed.
