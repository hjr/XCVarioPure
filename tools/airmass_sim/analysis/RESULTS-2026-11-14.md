# Integration as on airmass-dev10, 2026-11-14

Taken from `airmass-dev10`: the vario filter structure (the damped air mass vario takes the place of netto), the wind in
`inst_wind` / `synoptic_wind` with the degraded flag in the wind value, the flap object for every flapped glider, the
blue vario arrow, the labels. Two of these change what the pilot sees:

## Air mass needle damping

One low pass with tau = 0.6 × Vario Damping / 3 (0.8 s at the default 4 s) instead of the two stage damping with half
the Vario Damping. The estimator is unchanged.

- Simulator `nl`: cruise / thermal entry / circling rms 0.33 / 0.51 / 0.36 (0.34 / 0.51 / 0.36 before), display lag 1.4 s
  (1.4 s), TE netto 0.44 / 0.59 / 0.42 and 2.4 s.
- Real flights, 43 logs, cockpit needle over 10 s against the inertial GNSS reference, AirMass / TE netto: all 0.774 /
  1.069, straight 0.602 / 1.161, circling 0.974 / 1.055 (0.783 / 1.072, 0.603 / 1.161, 0.991 / 1.065 with the damping
  before on the same windows). The reference moved a little as well (next section), so this is no worse, not a gain.

## Wind average while the instant wind is degraded

The 30 s average now follows the degraded instant wind too, instead of restarting from it every cycle, and is published
as the synoptic wind. The estimator and the vario are unchanged.

43 logs, the average against the GNSS-only wind of each full circle (`gnssCircleWind`, residual up to 1 m/s):

| | circles with a valid wind | median error | circles with a degraded wind | median error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| before | 2224 | 1.25 m/s | 63 | 1.08 m/s |
| after | 2224 | 1.27 m/s | 63 | 1.23 m/s |

Of the free flight 48.7% has a valid and 40.1% a degraded instant wind: the gain is an average during those 40%, where
there was none; its accuracy there is hardly checked (degraded is straight flight, the check needs circles).
Simulator `nl`: 30 s average 0.45 m/s off the averaged truth as before.
